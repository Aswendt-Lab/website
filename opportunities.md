## Study how the brain recovers

We welcome inquiries from Bachelor and Master students interested in neuroscience, bioengineering, and MR physics.

Our research combines experimental stroke models, MRI, microscopy, and computational analysis. Explore our [research]({{ '/projects.html' | relative_url }}) and [software tools]({{ '/software.html' | relative_url }}) to find a direction that interests you.

## Discuss a student project

Contact **Prof. Dr. Markus Aswendt** to discuss current topics, supervision, and availability. In your message, please include:

- Your degree programme and current stage of study.
- The research question or method that interests you.
- Your relevant experience and a short CV.
- Your preferred start date and available time.

<a class="button" href="mailto:aswendt@med.uni-frankfurt.de?subject=Student%20project%20inquiry">Ask about a project</a>

Specific topics and requirements are agreed with the supervisor. You can also [meet the team]({{ '/team.html' | relative_url }}) and read our [publications]({{ '/paper.html' | relative_url }}).
